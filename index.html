Join FLaT

University of Greenwich · Student Society

Finance, law and the technology now running through both.

FLaT is a society for students who want to understand where financial services and legal practice are actually heading, not just where they've been.

We look at the two industries together, because increasingly the interesting problems sit at the point where they overlap: how regulation responds to AI, how contracts get automated, how financial products get built and policed. Alongside that, we run open sessions aimed at making both fields easier to understand full stop, whatever you're studying.

Join FLaT See what we do

FLaT exists because most finance and law societies treat the two as separate tracks, when in practice they're increasingly tangled together, and because it's easy to feel locked out of both if you don't already have a foot in the door.

We're not aiming this only at students who've already decided on a career in the City or a law firm. Some of our members have. Others just want to leave university able to read a set of accounts, understand a contract before they sign it, or hold a conversation about what AI regulation actually means in practice.

What ties the sessions together is a focus on the sectors as they're changing now, through the technology reshaping how financial and legal work actually gets done.

What we cover

Three articles, one overlap

§ Article I

Finance × Technology

How fintech, algorithmic trading and digital assets are changing what a career in financial services actually looks like. We cover open banking, RegTech and the shift toward AI-driven risk and compliance work, usually through sessions run with people currently doing the job.

§ Article II

Law × Technology

How legal practice is adapting to automation, AI governance and the regulatory questions that come with it. We look at legaltech tools, data protection and how firms are having to rethink advice and casework as the underlying technology changes.

§ Article III

Financial & legal literacy

Not everyone in the room is aiming for a career in finance or law, and that's fine. This strand covers the basics that are useful regardless of where you end up: how contracts work, what your rights actually are, how to read a payslip or a pension statement.

The programme

What actually happens at FLaT

A mix of formats, most of them open to non-members, all of them free.

FormatWhat it involves
Speaker sessions Practitioners from finance, law and technology talk about their actual day-to-day work, not just the highlights reel.
Workshops Smaller, practical sessions: case study work, mock negotiations, or building a simple financial model from scratch.
Literacy drop-ins Short, open sessions on a single practical topic. No prior knowledge needed and no obligation to join.
Employer contact Where we can arrange it, direct access to people currently working across the sectors we cover.

Why it exists

"I started FLaT because a lot of good, interesting content in this space assumes a level of access or background that not everyone has. It's meant to be a way in, whether you're already set on a career in finance or law or you just want to understand either a bit better."

Madison Farrell & Poulome Yadav — Co-Founders & Co-Presidents, FLaT Society

We're building the committee from scratch

FLaT is currently run by two co-presidents, and every other role on this list is open. If one of these sounds like you, get in touch, scope is flexible for a founding committee and roles can be shaped around who takes them on. See full role details and apply.

Open position

Vice President

Works alongside the co-presidents on strategy and keeps the committee running day to day. First point of contact for the wider committee.

Focus areas

  • Coordinating between pillar leads so the termly programme doesn't clash or overlap
  • Standing in for the co-presidents at SU meetings and events when needed
  • Planning committee handover and next year's elections

Open position

Treasurer

Owns the society's finances, from termly budgeting to keeping sponsors and the Students' Union satisfied that spend is tracked properly.

Focus areas

  • Managing the termly budget and processing expense claims
  • Applying for SU society funding and tracking sponsorship income
  • Reporting spend back to the committee each term

Open position

Secretary & Operations

Keeps the society organised: minutes, member records, room bookings, and the practical side of getting sessions to actually happen.

Focus areas

  • Booking rooms and setting up logistics for talks and workshops
  • Keeping attendance and membership records up to date
  • Taking minutes at committee meetings and circulating actions

Open position

Finance & Fintech Lead

Owns Article I of the programme: sourcing speakers and building relationships with people working in banking, asset management and fintech.

Focus areas

  • Booking speakers for finance and fintech sessions each term
  • Building a shortlist of contacts in banking, asset management and fintech
  • Designing at least one practical workshop a term, e.g. a valuation or trading exercise

Open position

Law & Legaltech Lead

Owns Article II of the programme: sourcing speakers and building relationships with people working in law and legal technology.

Focus areas

  • Booking speakers for law and legaltech sessions each term
  • Building a shortlist of contacts at firms and legal tech companies
  • Designing at least one practical workshop a term, e.g. a contract review or negotiation exercise

Open position

Literacy & Outreach Lead

Owns Article III: designs the drop-in literacy sessions and leads outreach to students who might not otherwise come across FLaT.

Focus areas

  • Planning termly literacy drop-ins on practical topics like contracts, payslips and budgeting
  • Building links with access and widening-participation groups on campus
  • Gathering feedback from non-member attendees to shape future sessions

Open position

Marketing & Communications Lead

Gets the word out. Runs the society's social channels, keeps the website current, and makes sure sessions actually get an audience.

Focus areas

  • Running FLaT's Instagram and LinkedIn
  • Keeping the website and event listings up to date
  • Designing promotional material for each session

Open position

Partnerships & Sponsorship Lead

Builds relationships with firms and organisations who can support FLaT with speakers, sponsorship or venue access.

Focus areas

  • Approaching firms for sponsorship and in-kind support
  • Maintaining relationships with existing partners term to term
  • Working with the pillar leads to convert contacts into speakers
