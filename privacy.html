Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your data

Last updated: 21 August 2026

1. Introduction

FLaT Society ("we", "us", or "our") operates the https://financelawandtech.netlify.app website. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use and disclosure of personal data when you use our site and the choices you have associated with that data.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect data only when you choose to provide it:

  • Email addresses — when you sign up to join FLaT or receive event updates
  • Names — when you apply for committee roles or submit forms
  • Optional contact info — Telegram or LinkedIn handles (if you choose to provide)
  • Form messages — optional text you submit with applications
  • Analytics data — via Google Analytics: page views, scroll depth, button clicks, device type

3. How We Use Your Data

Email and form data: Used only to contact you about your application or to send event announcements you've signed up for.

Analytics: To understand how users interact with our site, improve site performance, and measure the success of events and promotions.

Retention: We retain your email for as long as you remain an active member. You can request deletion at any time by emailing our co-presidents at mf9767b@gre.ac.uk and py2774p@greenwich.ac.uk.

4. Security Headers

Our site implements industry-standard security headers to protect against common web attacks, including:

  • Content Security Policy (CSP) — restricts where scripts and styles can load from
  • X-Frame-Options — prevents clickjacking attacks
  • X-Content-Type-Options — prevents MIME type sniffing
  • Strict-Transport-Security — enforces HTTPS-only connections

5. Form Protection

Our forms include spam protection measures to prevent automated abuse. If your submission is blocked, please try again or contact our co-presidents directly at mf9767b@gre.ac.uk and py2774p@greenwich.ac.uk.

6. Cookies & Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to track anonymous usage data. This includes:

  • Pages you visit
  • Time spent on the site
  • Device and browser type
  • General location (country-level, not precise)

Google Analytics uses cookies with the following settings:

  • IP addresses are anonymized
  • SameSite=None; Secure
  • No personally identifiable information is sent to Google

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — loads typefaces from Google servers
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage tracking
  • Netlify — hosts our website

Each has its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing them.

8. Your Rights

Under UK and EU data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent for data use

To exercise these rights, please contact us at mf9767b@gre.ac.uk and py2774p@greenwich.ac.uk with your request.

9. Legal Basis

We process your data based on:

  • Consent — when you sign up for emails or submit forms
  • Legitimate interest — to improve our site and understand member needs
  • Legal obligation — if required by law or University policy

10. Children's Privacy

Our site is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under this age. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it immediately.

11. Policy Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site means you accept the new policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, contact us at:

  • Co-president emails: mf9767b@gre.ac.uk and py2774p@greenwich.ac.uk
  • University of Greenwich

This privacy policy applies to the FLaT Society website. If you have concerns about how the University of Greenwich handles your data, contact their Data Protection Officer.
